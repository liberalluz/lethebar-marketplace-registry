# AI contributions

AI-assisted changes are allowed when the author can explain and verify them.

## Required

- A human reviews every AI-generated change.
- Tests or evidence are included.
- The author understands the diff.
- No secrets, customer data, or private material in prompts.

## Not allowed

- Merging an unreviewed AI change.
- Using AI to bypass a required check.
- Presenting generated text as verified evidence.
